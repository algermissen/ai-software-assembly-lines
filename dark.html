4. Progressive Dark Factory Lines

← Back to index

Description

Lights-out / dark factory concept from manufacturing (robots build with lights off, no humans on floor). Applied to software: fully automated agent pipelines from spec to production for domains where reliability is proven. Progressive and risk-based: start "lit" (human in loop) and darken only after demonstrated first-pass yield, low escaped defects, and acceptable cost-of-error. High-stakes (security, payments, core logic, irreversible changes) remain supervised. Humans focus on intent, architecture, and exception handling.

Manufacturing Parallel

Fanuc-style lights-out plants. Not everything is dark; risk and maturity determine autonomy level. 5-level autonomy scales (from assisted to fully hands-off) map well.

Adversarial Assessment

Pros: Scales with AI capability improvements. Matches cost of mistakes to oversight. Frees humans for high-leverage work. Practical near-term path (most teams at levels 2-3 today). Addresses poteto's continuous stream by controlling chaos where safe.

Cons / Risks: Hard to accurately measure "trustworthy enough." Misclassification of risk. Silent failures or regressions in dark zones as models/prompts drift. Potential skill atrophy. Over-optimism about full autonomy.

Viability: High practical near-term value. Must-have progressive approach rather than all-or-nothing.

Team Adversarial Vote: Essential. Implement with clear risk tiers and continuous monitoring. Do not force full dark factory prematurely.
