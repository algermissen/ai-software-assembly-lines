4. Progressive Dark Factory Lines — Detailed Implementation

← Index

Architecture Overview

Risk-tiered autonomy overlay on the assembly line and MES. Each work item and station has a risk classification. Dark (fully automated, no human gate) is allowed only when historical first-pass yield, escaped defect rate, and detection capability meet thresholds for that risk tier. Strong rollback, observability, and human override always available.

Technology Choices

  • Risk Classification: Fields on tickets/orders + policy engine (OPA/Rego or custom rules evaluating blast radius, reversibility, data sensitivity, historical performance of similar changes). Updated by humans or from incident data.
  • Autonomy Decision: MES or dedicated policy service checks current tier eligibility before each human-sensitive gate. Decision logged as event.
  • Progressive Promotion: Metrics service (SPC layer) computes rolling yield/defect rates per risk category and station; auto-suggests or requires human approval to raise autonomy level.
  • Safety Nets: Feature flags / progressive delivery (LaunchDarkly or custom), automatic rollback on anomaly detection, canary analysis. All events in UDN.
  • Human Override: Always possible via Andon or explicit hold; logged for Kaizen.

Defined Datapoints & Metrics

  • Per item: risk_tier (low/medium/high/critical), current_autonomy_level (lit/supervised/dark), eligibility_score (based on historical yield for similar items).
  • Thresholds: e.g., dark allowed if first-pass yield > 95% and escaped defects < 0.5% over last N items in that category.
  • Metrics: % of volume running dark by tier, promotion/demotion events, silent failure rate in dark zones, mean time to human intervention when needed, skill coverage (human practice on high-risk).

Development / Operations Process Flow

  1. Order enters with risk classification (manual or auto-suggested).
  2. At each potential human gate, policy evaluates: if eligible for dark and station history supports it, skip human and proceed; else require review.
  3. All steps still emit full events and traces for later analysis.
  4. Post-deploy monitoring runs; anomalies can auto-demote the category or trigger rollback.
  5. Periodic review (or continuous SPC) of dark-zone performance; promote reliable categories, demote if drift detected.
  6. Humans retain ultimate authority and focus on defining constraints, reviewing exceptions, and improving the system.

Enables safe scaling of the continuous code stream while protecting high-stakes work. Autonomy is earned and continuously re-validated, not assumed.
