4. Progressive Dark Factory Lines — Detailed Implementation

← Back to index

Architecture Overview

Risk-tiered autonomy overlay. Dark only after demonstrated reliability for the risk tier. Strong rollback and override always available.

Technology Choices

  • Risk classification via policy engine (OPA/Rego).
  • Progressive promotion based on SPC signals.
  • Safety nets: feature flags, canary, auto-rollback.

Defined Datapoints & Metrics

  • risk_tier, autonomy_level, eligibility_score, first-pass yield thresholds, escape rate.

Development / Operations Process Flow

  1. Classify risk on entry.
  2. Policy decides human gate or dark at each step.
  3. Full events always emitted.
  4. Monitor and demote on anomalies; promote on sustained yield.
