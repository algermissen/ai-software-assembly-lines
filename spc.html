Architecture Overview

Measurement layer computing OEE and SPC from UDN/OTel data, driving Kaizen.

Technology Choices

  • OpenTelemetry metrics/traces to Prometheus or ClickHouse.
  • Grafana dashboards and alerting.
  • Control charts for process stability.

Defined Datapoints & Metrics

OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality

  • Availability: uptime / planned time
  • Performance: ideal cycle / actual
  • Quality: first-pass yield
  • Plus: rework rate, escaped defects, cycle time by stage, Cpk-style capability.

Development / Operations Process Flow

  1. Instrument all stations/agents.
  2. Compute rolling OEE and control charts.
  3. Out-of-control signals raise Andon or Kaizen candidates.
  4. Use for data-driven improvement, not vanity metrics.
